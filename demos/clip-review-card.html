How this should feel

One clip at a time. Watch it, crop it, discard it if you do not want it. Everything you do not discard is kept. When you are done, hit the large Save button.

Trim

  • No more ±1s buttons. Drag the orange handles on the bar under the video.
  • The white line is where you are. Click the bar to scrub.
  • Times on the left and right still accept a typed value if you want it exact.

Crop & zoom

  • Original keeps the full frame. 4:3 / 9:16 / 1:1 put a window on the video.
  • Drag inside the window to move it.
  • Drag a corner, use + / −, or scroll to zoom. The control sits on the picture, same style as play.

Copy from the last clip

  • Set 9:16 on clip 1 and place the window on the speaker.
  • Go to clip 2 and tap 9:16 again — same place and zoom come over.
Try this: drag the right handle a little, then pick 9:16, punch in with +, and go to the next clip and tap 9:16 again.
